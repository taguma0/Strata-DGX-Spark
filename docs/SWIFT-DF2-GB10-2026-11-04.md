the neutral measurement: it is the explicit-only form (Auto keeps the MTP, so every default start,

in the repo root starts DFlash2 alone on the fine-tune. A conf sweep (0.5 / 0.65 / 0.8)
and code-tuned prompts remain unexplored; upstream measured DFlash2 +/- by text kind.
